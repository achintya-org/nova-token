FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the presale live?

Yes — deposits sent from the buy widget go directly to the presale wallet published above. The NOVA token contract itself hasn't been deployed yet; tokens are distributed at TGE.

Which wallets are supported?

Any browser wallet that injects an Ethereum provider, such as MetaMask, works out of the box.

Which network should I use?

. If you're on a different network, the page will prompt you to switch.

Where can I find the presale wallet address?

It's published above and on our official channels. Always double-check it before sending funds — never trust an address from anywhere else.
